# KAŌN brand narrative

Owner direction, 2026-11-07. The interface stays English.

## Core message

**Your ideas. On-chain evidence.**

**DeFi lab · AI research · SocialFi**

A workspace to research crypto, test hypotheses and explore trading strategies.
Keep the evidence on your board. Share what holds up. Stay in control of your wallet.

## Supporting lines

- Research. Test. Share.
- Your wallet. Your decisions.
- Bring your next hypothesis.
- Non-custodial by design. Sources and assumptions in view.

Russian editorial equivalent (for Russian communication):
**Ваши идеи. Доказательства в блокчейне.**
Рабочее пространство для DeFi-экспериментов, AI-исследований и SocialFi:
изучайте активы, проверяйте гипотезы и стратегии, обсуждайте результаты.
Ваш кошелёк и решения остаются под вашим контролем.

## The three pillars

| Pillar | What a user can do today |
| --- | --- |
| AI research | Ask about assets, wallets and protocols; inspect cited sources and figures marked measured, estimated or judged; arrange and compare answer cards. |
| DeFi lab | Compare DCA, buy-and-hold and moving-average strategies on historical daily prices; explore what-if scenarios and route quotes; use supported pool tools with wallet approval. |
| SocialFi | Publish cards under a stable pseudonym; discuss, vote, star and build reputation on the Wall; tip authors directly wallet to wallet. |

## Trust means observable behavior

- Research and quotes do not send trades. Supported on-chain actions require review and signature in the user's wallet. KAŌN does not hold private keys.
- Historical strategy comparisons exclude fees, slippage and taxes; they are hindsight, not forecasts. A quote is not a fill or a profit guarantee.
- Show sources, assumptions, data dates and limitations. Contracts can be verified on Etherscan. Do not call the private application open source.
- Non-custodial does not remove market, wallet, protocol or smart-contract risks. Never promise absolute safety or guaranteed returns.
- SocialFi does not imply posting rewards, yield, copy trading or autonomous execution.
- Sources include on-chain, exchange, market and news data. The slogan expresses an evidence-led approach; it must not relabel every source as on-chain.
- Not financial advice. Demo and captured answers remain identified.
